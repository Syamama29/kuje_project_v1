About the Chairman

Hon. Danjuma Samuel Shekwolo

Executive Chairman, Kuje Area Council, FCT Abuja

Political Party All Progressives Congress (APC)
Sworn Into Office June 16, 2026
Election Date February 21, 2026
Votes Secured 17,269 Votes

Hon. Danjuma Samuel Shekwolo is a seasoned political leader with vast experience, exposure, and a strong ideological footprint. Described by political stakeholders as a consensus builder, he emerged as the APC's unanimous candidate for the Kuje Area Council chairmanship after seven other aspirants voluntarily stepped down for him during the June 2025 primaries—a testament to his widespread popularity and track record of service across the Federal Capital Territory.

His leadership philosophy is anchored on servant leadership, transparent governance, and a relentless commitment to grassroots development. Since assuming office, he has centered his administration around President Bola Ahmed Tinubu's "Renewed Hope Agenda," emphasizing that "Renewed Hope is not a slogan but an action."

The Mandate

Election Details & Inauguration

The historic election that brought Hon. Shekwolo to office as the Executive Chairman of Kuje Area Council.

🗳️

The Election

Hon. Shekwolo won the Kuje Area Council chairmanship election held on Saturday, February 21, 2026, in a competitive and peaceful electoral process.

🏆

The Results

17,269

He was declared the winner on February 22, 2026, by the INEC Returning Officer, Prof. Nkiruka Odoh of the University of Abuja, having secured a decisive victory over his rivals.

🎖️

Swearing-In Ceremony

He was officially sworn into office on June 16, 2026, alongside other FCT Area Council chairmen, marking the beginning of a new era of leadership for Kuje.

The Agenda

Vision, Mission & Core Agenda

The guiding principles and priorities of the Shekwolo administration for Kuje Area Council.

🇳🇬

The "Renewed Hope" Alignment

Shekwolo has centered his administration's mission around President Bola Ahmed Tinubu's "Renewed Hope Agenda." During his inauguration, he emphasized that "Renewed Hope is not a slogan but an action," signaling a hands-on approach to local governance that prioritizes tangible results over rhetoric.

🤝

Collaboration for Infrastructure

His vision relies heavily on inter-governmental collaboration. He pledged to work closely with the FCT Minister, Nyesom Wike, to attract major infrastructure projects to Kuje and ensure the rapid delivery of basic amenities such as roads, water supply, and electricity to rural communities across all 10 wards.

💼

Economic Empowerment

A primary focus of his mandate is tackling youth and female unemployment, reducing poverty, and driving sustainable economic empowerment at the grassroots level. His administration aims to create opportunities through skills training, small business support, and agricultural value chain development.

🌱

Grassroots Development

His agenda includes driving sociopolitical and economic progress through local initiatives, such as supporting sports programs like the Kuje Football League to engage youths and stimulate the local economy. The council under his leadership prioritizes community-driven development projects.

Delivering Results

Recent Actions in Office

Since assuming office, Hon. Shekwolo has hit the ground running with impactful initiatives.

July 2026

Skills Acquisition Program for Women & Youths

Hon. Shekwolo flagged off a comprehensive skills acquisition program specifically targeted at women and youths in Kuje Area Council. During the launch, he reaffirmed his administration's commitment to equipping residents with the necessary vocational and entrepreneurial skills to combat unemployment and drive self-reliance.

The program covers training in tailoring, ICT, carpentry, catering, and modern agricultural techniques, with participants receiving starter kits upon completion.

Ongoing Initiative

Kuje Football League & Youth Sports Development

Recognizing the power of sports in youth engagement and community development, the Chairman has thrown his weight behind the Kuje Football League and other sporting initiatives. These programs provide a platform for young talents to showcase their skills while promoting unity and healthy competition among communities.

The council has committed to upgrading local sports facilities and organizing regular tournaments to discover and nurture sporting talents across the 10 wards.
