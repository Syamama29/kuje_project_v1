Understanding Our Structure

Political Wards of Kuje Area Council

Kuje Area Council is divided into 10 political wards, which serve as the foundational units of local governance and political representation. Each ward is represented by an elected councilor who serves in the Kuje Legislative Council, voicing the concerns and aspirations of their constituents.

These wards encompass numerous towns, villages, and settlements spread across the council's 1,644 square kilometers of land area. From the bustling Kuje Central to the agricultural heartlands of Gaube and Rubochi, each ward contributes uniquely to the council's identity as the "Food Basket of the FCT."

Legislative Representation

Ward Councilors & Legislative Council

Each of the 10 political wards elects a councilor who represents the community's interests in the Kuje Legislative Council. The councilors work alongside the Executive Chairman to enact by-laws, approve budgets, and oversee development projects.

🏛️

The Kuje Legislative Council

The Legislative Council comprises 10 elected councilors representing each ward, led by the Speaker of the Council. The council meets regularly at the Kuje Secretariat to deliberate on matters affecting the welfare of residents, approve annual budgets, and provide oversight on executive projects across all wards.

Councilors maintain constituency offices within their respective wards, ensuring that every resident has direct access to their elected representative for grievances, requests, and community development proposals.
