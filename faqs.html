The Kuje Area Council Secretariat operates from Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. The secretariat is closed on weekends (Saturdays and Sundays) and all federally recognized public holidays.

For urgent matters outside regular hours, please contact our emergency lines listed on the Contact page.

You can reach the Executive Chairman's office through the following channels:

  • Phone:+2347015676309 (Executive Enquiries)
  • Email: chairman@kujeareacouncil.gov.ng
  • In Person: Visit the Secretariat during office hours and request an appointment at the Executive Chairman's office.

For general inquiries, use our Contact Form and select the appropriate department.

The Kuje Area Council Secretariat is located at:

Secretariat Road, Kuje, Federal Capital Territory, Abuja, Nigeria.

The secretariat is easily accessible from the Kuje Main Market and is a landmark building in the town center. You can view the exact location on our Contact page map.

You can pay your tenement rates and council taxes through the following methods:

  • In Person: Visit the Revenue Department at the Secretariat with your payment assessment notice.
  • Bank Deposit: Pay into the official Kuje Area Council revenue account at any designated commercial bank.
  • Online: Visit our E-Services portal to make secure online payments.

Important: Always collect an official receipt for every payment made. Verify that the receipt bears the Kuje Area Council official stamp and seal.

The official Approved Revenue Rates list is available for download:

  • PDF Download: Download Tax Guidelines (PDF) from our website footer or resources section.
  • Physical Copy: Available at the Revenue Department, Kuje Secretariat during office hours.

The rates are updated annually. Always refer to the latest version for current charges.

To verify the identity of a revenue collection officer:

  • ID Card: All legitimate officers carry a photo ID card issued by Kuje Area Council with a unique identification number.
  • Uniform: Official collectors wear designated council uniforms with Kuje Area Council branding.
  • Verification: Call the Revenue Department at +234 9 000 0001 to confirm the officer's identity before making any payment.

Warning: Never pay cash to anyone who cannot produce valid council identification. Report suspicious collectors immediately.

To apply for a Business Premises Permit:

  1. Visit the Works & Housing Department at the Secretariat.
  2. Obtain and complete the Business Premises Permit application form.
  3. Submit required documents: proof of ownership/tenancy, business registration certificate, and passport photographs.
  4. Pay the prescribed fee at the Revenue Department.
  5. A site inspection will be scheduled by council officials.
  6. Upon approval, collect your permit from the Works & Housing Department.

Processing time is typically 5-10 working days after submission of complete documents.

For Trade Licenses and Market Stall allocation:

  • Trade License: Apply through the Revenue Department with your business details. Pay the applicable fee based on your business category.
  • Market Stall: Visit the Market Management Office at your preferred market (e.g., Kuje Main Market). Complete the allocation form and pay the stall fee. Allocation is subject to availability.

Both processes require valid identification and may involve a waiting period depending on demand.

Registration of vital events is handled by the Health & Social Development Department:

  • Birth Registration: Bring the hospital notification of birth, parents' identification, and witnesses. Registration is free for infants under 1 year.
  • Marriage Registration: Both parties must appear in person with valid ID, passport photographs, and witnesses. A statutory fee applies.

Visit the Secretariat during office hours or contact us for more details.

Kuje Area Council manages several primary health centers across the 10 wards, including:

  • Kuje Primary Health Care Center – Main facility near the Secretariat
  • Kayarda Primary Health Clinic
  • Gaube Primary Health Post
  • Other ward-level health posts serving rural communities

Services include maternal care, immunization, outpatient treatment, and health education. All centers operate on weekdays with emergency services available at the main center.

Farmers in Kuje Area Council can access agricultural support through:

  • Agriculture & Natural Resources Department: Register as a farmer at the Secretariat to be included in support programs.
  • Fertilizer Distribution: Subsidized fertilizer is distributed seasonally. Listen for announcements or visit the department for schedules.
  • Extension Services: Council agricultural officers provide training and technical support to registered farmers.

Bring proof of farmland (community leader's letter) and valid ID when registering.

Kuje Area Council runs regular youth empowerment initiatives:

  • Registration: Visit the Education & Social Development Department at the Secretariat to register your interest.
  • Programs Offered: Vocational skills training (tailoring, carpentry, ICT), entrepreneurship workshops, and apprenticeship placements.
  • Eligibility: Open to Kuje residents aged 18-35. Priority is given to unemployed youth.

Follow our News page for announcements on upcoming empowerment programs and application deadlines.
