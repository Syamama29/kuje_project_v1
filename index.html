Leadership Message

Welcome to Kuje Area Council

"Our mandate is to foster rapid socio-economic growth, grassroots development, and public welfare across every ward in Kuje. We are committed to transparent governance and inclusive progress."

Welcome to the official digital portal of Kuje Area Council. Whether you are a resident, business owner, or visitor, our platform is designed to make access to local government administration and municipal services seamless and transparent. Together, we are building a community that works for everyone.
