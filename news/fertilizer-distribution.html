Council Distributes Fertilizers to 5,000 Registered Farmers Across 10 Wards

Kuje Area Council supports the agricultural sector as part of the administration's food security initiative, strengthening its position as the Food Basket of the FCT.

Home › News & Events › Fertilizer Distribution
🌾 Agriculture 📅 June 25, 2026 👤 Agriculture & Natural Resources Dept. ⏱️ 4 min read

🌾 Key Highlights

  • 5,000 Farmers Benefited: Subsidized fertilizers distributed across all 10 wards
  • Food Security Initiative: Supporting Kuje's role as the Food Basket of the FCT
  • Subsidized Rates: Fertilizers provided at below-market prices
  • Registered Farmers: Distribution targeted verified and registered farmers

The Kuje Area Council has successfully distributed subsidized fertilizers to 5,000 registered farmers across all 10 wards, marking a significant investment in the agricultural sector and reaffirming the council's commitment to food security in the Federal Capital Territory.

The distribution exercise, led by the Agriculture & Natural Resources Department under the direction of Hon. Danjuma Samuel Shekwolo, is part of the administration's comprehensive agricultural support program designed to boost crop production and improve the livelihoods of farming communities.

Distribution Process

The fertilizer distribution followed a transparent and equitable process to ensure that only genuine farmers benefited:

  1. Farmer Registration: Farmers registered through their ward agricultural officers
  2. Verification: Registration details verified by the Agriculture Department
  3. Ward Allocation: Fertilizer quotas allocated based on farming population
  4. Distribution Centers: Designated centers established in each ward
  5. Collection: Registered farmers collected their allocation with identification

Distribution Details

The subsidized fertilizer program included various types to meet different crop needs:

🌱

NPK Fertilizer

Balanced fertilizer for general crop production

🌿

Urea Fertilizer

High-nitrogen fertilizer for cereal crops

🍅

Organic Compost

Environment-friendly option for vegetable farming

🌾

Crop-Specific Blends

Specialized formulations for maize, rice, and yam

Support for Farmers

Beyond fertilizer distribution, the Kuje Area Council provides comprehensive support to farmers:

  • Extension Services: Agricultural officers provide technical advice and training
  • Improved Seedlings: Access to high-yield seed varieties
  • Market Access: Support connecting farmers to markets in Abuja
  • Storage Facilities: Development of community storage infrastructure
  • Farm Inputs: Access to herbicides, pesticides, and farm tools

"Agriculture is the backbone of our local economy. By supporting our farmers with subsidized inputs and technical assistance, we are not just growing crops—we are growing prosperity for our communities and ensuring food security for the Federal Capital Territory."

Hon. Danjuma Samuel Shekwolo Executive Chairman, Kuje Area Council, FCT Abuja

Beneficiary farmers expressed their gratitude for the council's support:

Food Security Impact

The fertilizer distribution program is expected to have a significant impact on food production:

  • Increased Crop Yields: Expected 30-40% increase in crop production
  • Reduced Food Prices: Higher supply leading to more affordable food
  • Job Creation: Expansion of farming activities creating employment
  • Rural Development: Improved incomes for farming communities
  • Food Sufficiency: Contributing to FCT's food security goals

Future Agricultural Programs

The council has outlined additional agricultural initiatives for the coming seasons:

  • Mechanization Program: Provision of tractors and farm machinery for cooperatives
  • Irrigation Development: Construction of small-scale irrigation schemes
  • Farmer Training: Capacity building on modern farming techniques
  • Market Infrastructure: Development of rural markets and storage facilities
  • Youth in Agriculture: Program to encourage young people into farming

For more information about agricultural support programs or to register as a farmer, contact the Agriculture & Natural Resources Department or visit the Contact page.
