Transforming Kuje: 65 Days of Impactful Governance

Hon. Danjuma Samuel Shekwolo's administration delivers unprecedented achievements across infrastructure, youth empowerment, healthcare, and staff welfare in just 65 days.

Home › News & Events › Transforming Kuje
🏗️ Infrastructure 📅 August 12, 2026 👤 Council Media Office ⏱️ 5 min read

🔑 Key Achievements at a Glance

  • 108 Youths enrolled in Nigerian Army - highest single-intake in Kuje history
  • 100+ Youths trained in 7 skills acquisition programs
  • Kuje Water System revival underway for clean water access
  • Staff Welfare - 2023 promotions and 2025 increments implemented
  • Free healthcare - eye care outreach and anti-rabies vaccination drives

In just 65 days in office, the Executive Chairman of Kuje Area Council, Hon. Danjuma Samuel Shekwolo, has demonstrated visionary leadership, decisive action, and an unwavering commitment to sustainable development and human empowerment. Through strategic initiatives cutting across infrastructure, healthcare, capacity building, and youth development, the administration is rapidly laying down the blueprint for a progressive Kuje Area Council.

1. Infrastructure & Environmental Sanitation

The administration has prioritized critical infrastructure projects that directly impact the daily lives of residents. Major achievements include:

💧

Revival of the Kuje Water System

Comprehensive restoration efforts are actively ongoing to ensure clean, reliable water access for all residents across the council area.

🧹

Routine Waste Management

Continuous evacuation of refuse dumps across key locations including Kuje Stadium, Pasali/Pappu Junction, and major metropolis hotspots.

🛣️

Community Access Road

Construction of an alternative access road to improve mobility and connectivity for the people of the Shetugo Community.

2. Youth Empowerment & Employment

Addressing youth unemployment has been a cornerstone of the Shekwolo administration. The achievements in this area are unprecedented:

Historic Military Recruitment

The administration facilitated the successful enrollment of 108 local youths into the Nigerian Army — the highest single-intake figure in the history of Kuje Area Council. Candidates are currently undergoing rigorous training across three military depots.

Skills Acquisition Programme

Over 100 youths have been empowered across the council with practical, market-ready training in seven skill areas:

💇

Barbing & Beauty Care

Professional training in barbing, make-up artistry, and beauty care services.

👜

Bag Making & Craftsmanship

Hands-on training in leatherwork, bag production, and artisanal craftsmanship.

🌬️

Room Air Freshener Production

Practical training in the production of home care and air freshener products.

💻

Computer Literacy & ICT

Digital skills training covering basic computing, internet usage, and office applications.

🍳

Catering & Culinary Arts

Professional culinary training for aspiring chefs and catering entrepreneurs.

🧵

Tailoring & Fashion Design

Comprehensive training in garment making, pattern drafting, and fashion entrepreneurship.

📹

Videography & Film Photography

Training in video production, photography, and digital media content creation.

3. Public Health & Community Welfare

The administration has demonstrated a strong commitment to the health and wellbeing of Kuje residents through targeted public health interventions.

👁️

Free Eye Care Outreach

Organized comprehensive free eye screenings and cataract surgeries for vulnerable community members, restoring sight and improving quality of life.

🐕

Public Safety Initiative

Conducted free anti-rabies vaccination drives to protect households and livestock across the council from the threat of rabies.

4. Governance, Legislative & Capacity Building

Strengthening the institutional capacity of the council has been a priority, ensuring that governance structures are equipped for effective service delivery.

📋

Legislative Orientation Workshop

Hosted an exclusive capacity-building retreat for Councilors, Clerks, and legislative officers to foster legislative efficiency and effectiveness.

🎓

Executive Leadership Training

Conducted a specialized development workshop for Supervisory Councilors and Senior Special Assistants (SSAs) to enhance executive capacity.

5. Civil Service & Staff Welfare

The administration has prioritized the welfare of council staff, implementing long-awaited benefits and ensuring that workers receive their entitlements.

📈

2023 Promotion Implementation

Officially implemented long-awaited promotional advancements for eligible staff, providing career progression opportunities.

💰

2025 Annual Increment

Successfully applied the annual step-increment for council workers in line with civil service regulations.

✅

Allowance Clearances

Active disbursement and ongoing clearance of peculiar allowances and outstanding arrears owed to council staff.

A new era of accountable leadership, human development, and structural growth has begun in Kuje Area Council.

Hon. Danjuma Samuel Shekwolo Executive Chairman, Kuje Area Council, FCT Abuja

A New Era of Leadership

The first 65 days of the Shekwolo administration have set a high bar for governance in Kuje Area Council. With a clear vision, decisive action, and a commitment to the Renewed Hope Agenda, the council is on a transformative path that promises sustainable development for all residents.

As the administration continues to roll out its developmental agenda across all 10 wards, residents can expect continued improvements in infrastructure, service delivery, and quality of life. The foundation has been laid for a progressive, prosperous, and well-governed Kuje Area Council.
