Our Story

History & Overview of Kuje Area Council

Kuje Area Council was established as one of the six Area Councils in Nigeria's Federal Capital Territory (FCT), Abuja. Created to bring governance closer to the grassroots, Kuje has grown from a modest rural settlement into a vibrant and strategically important local government area.

Fondly referred to as the "Food Basket of the FCT," Kuje boasts vast arable land that supports large-scale farming and agricultural production. The area is renowned for its rich cultural heritage, with diverse ethnic groups including the indigenous Gbagyi people, Hausa, Fulani, and other Nigerian communities living together in harmony.

Strategically located near the Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport, Kuje serves as a gateway between the Federal Capital City and the surrounding rural communities. With a land area of approximately 1,644 square kilometers and a population exceeding 300,000 residents, Kuje continues to experience rapid growth and urbanization.
